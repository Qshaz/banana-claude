AR-RA'D · 13:28
أَلَا بِذِكْرِ اللَّهِ تَطْمَئِنُّ الْقُلُوبُ
"Verily, in the remembrance of Allah do hearts find rest."
Mishari Rashid al-Afasy
📚 Ibn Kathir Tafsir▼
Ibn Kathir explains that this verse refers to the hearts of the believers finding tranquility and peace through the dhikr (remembrance) of Allah. This includes recitation of the Quran, glorification, supplication, and all forms of worship. The word tatma'inn (تَطْمَئِنُّ) means to become still, settled, and at rest — as opposed to the restless anxiety that comes from heedlessness.

The asbab al-nuzul: This verse was revealed in a passage addressing those who asked for a sign from the Prophet ﷺ. Allah responded that guidance has already been given, and that the believers — by turning to Allah — experience the heart's rest that is itself the greatest sign.

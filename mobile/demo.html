AR-RA'D · 13:28
أَلَا بِذِكْرِ اللَّهِ تَطْمَئِنُّ الْقُلُوبُ
"Verily, in the remembrance of Allah do hearts find rest."
▶  Listen · Mishari Rashid al-Afasy
Ibn Kathir explains that this verse refers to the hearts of believers finding tranquility through dhikr (remembrance) of Allah — which includes recitation of the Quran, glorification, and all acts of worship. The word tatma'inn (تَطْمَئِنُّ) means to become settled and at rest, as opposed to the anxiety that comes from heedlessness of Allah.

The occasion of revelation: This verse was revealed addressing those who demanded miraculous signs. Allah's response was that guidance has already come — and the believers who turn to Allah experience the heart's rest that is itself the greatest sign.

Ibn Kathir cites the hadith of the Prophet ﷺ: "Truly in the body there is a piece of flesh: if it is sound, the whole body is sound, and if it is corrupt, the whole body is corrupt. Truly it is the heart." (Bukhari & Muslim)

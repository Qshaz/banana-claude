# Tadabur Life Categories

Category-to-search-term mapping for verse discovery. Each entry includes:
- Arabic transliteration and script
- English search terms (for Server A `search_quran`)
- Arabic search terms (for Server B `ayah_search`)
- Key themes to look for in results
- Anchor verses (well-known, high-relevance starting points)

---

## 1. Patience — الصبر (As-Sabr)

**English terms:** patience, steadfast, persevere, endure, trials, tribulation, hardship  
**Arabic terms:** صبر، صابرين، اصبروا، ابتلاء، مصيبة

**Key themes:** enduring difficulty without despair, Allah's company with the patient, reward of patience, types of patience (on obedience, from sin, with decree)

**Anchor verses:**
- Al-Baqarah 2:153 — "O you who believe, seek help through patience and prayer..."
- Al-Baqarah 2:155-157 — "We will certainly test you... give good news to the steadfast..."
- Az-Zumar 39:10 — "The steadfast will be given their reward without limit..."
- Al-Imran 3:200 — "O believers, be steadfast, and outdo others in steadfastness..."

---

## 2. Hope — الرجاء والأمل (Ar-Raja wal-Amal)

**English terms:** hope, mercy, forgiveness, turn to Allah, return, despair not, rahma  
**Arabic terms:** رجاء، أمل، رحمة، توبة، لا تقنطوا، يرجو

**Key themes:** Allah's vast mercy, no despair for the believer, hope as worship, return after sin

**Anchor verses:**
- Az-Zumar 39:53 — "Do not despair of Allah's mercy..."
- Al-Hijr 15:56 — "Who despairs of his Lord's mercy except those who are astray?"
- Al-Baqarah 2:286 — "Allah does not burden a soul beyond what it can bear..."
- At-Talaq 65:3 — "Whoever puts their trust in Allah, He will be enough for them..."

---

## 3. Hopelessness / Despair — اليأس والقنوط (Al-Ya's wal-Qunut)

**English terms:** despair, hopeless, lost, no way out, overwhelmed, give up  
**Arabic terms:** يأس، قنوط، لا تقنطوا، يقنط، ضاق

**Key themes:** Allah explicitly forbids despair, stories of prophets who hit rock bottom and were rescued (Ayyub, Yunus, Yaqub)

**Anchor verses:**
- Az-Zumar 39:53 — "Do not despair of Allah's mercy; Allah forgives all sins..."
- Yusuf 12:87 — "Do not lose hope in Allah's mercy — only disbelievers lose hope..."
- Al-Inshirah 94:5-6 — "With every hardship comes ease. With every hardship comes ease."
- Al-Baqarah 2:216 — "You may dislike something which is good for you..."

---

## 4. Anxiety & Worry — القلق والخوف (Al-Qalaq wal-Khawf)

**English terms:** anxiety, worry, fear, troubled, distress, peace of heart, tranquility, reassurance  
**Arabic terms:** قلق، خوف، اطمئنان، تطمئن القلوب، حزن، ضيق

**Key themes:** remembrance of Allah as cure for anxiety, heart's peace through dhikr, Allah's reassurance to prophets who were anxious

**Anchor verses:**
- Ar-Ra'd 13:28 — "Verily, in the remembrance of Allah do hearts find rest."
- Al-Fajr 89:27-28 — "O tranquil soul, return to your Lord well-pleased, well-pleasing."
- Ta-Ha 20:46 — "Fear not. I am with you both, hearing and seeing."
- Al-Baqarah 2:286 — "Allah does not burden a soul beyond what it can bear..."

## 5. Depression & Grief — الحزن والاكتئاب (Al-Huzn wal-Ikti'ab)

**Arabic terms:** حزن، غم، حزين، اكتئاب، ألم، مصيبة

**Key themes:** Allah consoles the Prophet in grief, grief is not weakness, it is a sign of caring; Allah's nearness in pain; light after darkness

**Anchor verses:**
- Duha 93:3-5 — "Your Lord has not forsaken you, nor does He hate you..."
- Al-Inshirah 94:1-4 — "Did We not relieve your heart..."
- Yusuf 12:86 — "I only complain of my anguish and sorrow to Allah..."
- Al-Baqarah 2:286 — "Allah does not burden a soul..."

## 6. Stress & Overwhelm — الضغط والإرهاق (Ad-Daghd wal-Irhaq)

**English terms:** stress, overwhelmed, pressure, burden, too much, exhausted, weary  
**Arabic terms:** ضغط، إرهاق، حمل، ثقل، ضيق الصدر، كربة

**Key themes:** Allah takes away burdens, relief comes from Him, no hardship is permanent

**Anchor verses:**
- Al-Inshirah 94:1-8 — "Did We not relieve your chest..."
- Al-Baqarah 2:286 — "Allah does not burden a soul beyond its capacity..."

## 7. Trust in Allah — التوكل (At-Tawakkul)

**English terms:** trust, reliance, depend on Allah, put trust, sufficient, tawakkul  
**Arabic terms:** توكل، توكلوا، يتوكل، وكيل، حسبنا الله، كفى

**Key themes:** tawakkul as an active state, Allah suffices whoever trusts Him, stories of tawakkul (Maryam, Ibrahim, the birds)

**Anchor verses:**
- At-Talaq 65:3 — "Whoever puts their trust in Allah, He will be enough for them..."
- Al-Imran 3:159 — "...and when you have decided, put your trust in Allah..."
- Al-Mujadila 58:10 — "...put your trust in Allah — in Allah the believers should put their trust."
- At-Tawbah 9:129 — "Allah is sufficient for me; there is no god but Him. I put my trust in Him..."

## 8. Love & Longing for Allah — الحب (Al-Hubb)

**English terms:** love, beloved, closeness to Allah, longing, devotion, heart, love of dunya  
**Arabic terms:** حب، محبة، يحبهم ويحبونه، قرب، أحبه، والذين آمنوا أشد حباً

**Key themes:** Allah's love for His servants, the believer's love for Allah above all, love as a test, closeness through dhikr

**Anchor verses:**
- Al-Maidah 5:54 — "He will love them and they will love Him..."
- Al-Baqarah 2:165 — "Those who believe are strongest in love for Allah..."
- Al-Imran 3:31 — "Say: if you love Allah, follow me, and Allah will love you..."
- Qaf 50:16 — "We created man and know what his soul whispers — We are closer to him than his jugular vein."

**Arabic terms:** شكر، اشكروا، شاكرين، نعمة، فضل، ألم أعدد

**Key themes:** gratitude multiplies blessings, ingratitude is injustice, seeing blessings in hardship

**Anchor verses:**

- An-Nahl 16:18 — "If you tried to count Allah's blessings you would never number them..."
- Ar-Rahman 55 — (The entire surah) "Which of your Lord's favours would you deny?"

## 10. Financial Hardship & Rizq — الرزق والفقر (Ar-Rizq wal-Faqr)

**Arabic terms:** رزق، يرزق، فقر، فاقة، قوت، عيش، نفقة

**Key themes:** Allah is the Provider, rizq is decreed, du'a for provision, dignity in need, halal rizq

**Anchor verses:**
- Ad-Dhariyat 51:58 — "Allah is the All-Provider, the Lord of Power, the Firm."
- Hud 11:6 — "There is no creature on earth whose provision is not Allah's responsibility..."
- At-Talaq 65:3 — "Whoever puts their trust in Allah, He will be enough for them. Allah achieves His purpose..."
- Al-Baqarah 2:268 — "Satan threatens you with poverty and orders indecency; Allah promises forgiveness and bounty..."

## 11. Childlessness — الذرية والعقم (Adh-Dhurriyya wal-'Aqm)

**English terms:** childlessness, infertility, longing for children, barren, offspring, du'a for children  
**Arabic terms:** ذرية، ولد، أولاد، عقم، لا يولد له، نسل، هب لي

**Key themes:** Zakariyya's prayer, Hannah's prayer for Maryam, patience with this specific trial, children as trust (amanah), Allah's gift of children or of something greater

**Anchor verses:**
- Al-Anbiya 21:89-90 — Zakariyya's prayer: "My Lord, do not leave me childless..."
- Al-Imran 3:35-38 — Hannah's prayer and the birth of Maryam
- Ash-Shura 42:49-50 — "To Allah belongs the dominion of the heavens and earth. He creates what He wills: He gives daughters to whom He wills, sons to whom He wills..."
- Maryam 19:4-6 — Zakariyya: "My bones have grown feeble... grant me an heir from Yourself..."

## 12. Widowhood & Loss of a Spouse — الترمل (At-Tarammul)

**English terms:** widow, widower, loss of husband, loss of wife, alone after death of spouse  
**Arabic terms:** ترمل، وفاة الزوج، فراق، وحدة، توفي عنها زوجها

**Key themes:** Khadijah as model of strength, Allah's comfort in bereavement, new beginnings, iddah and its wisdom, hope in reunion in Jannah

**Anchor verses:**
- Al-Baqarah 2:234 — Rulings on iddah for widows, with implicit compassion in the verse
- Al-Baqarah 2:286 — "Allah does not burden a soul beyond what it can bear..."
- Duha 93 — Allah's comfort when alone and burdened
- Ar-Ra'd 13:28 — "In the remembrance of Allah hearts find peace."
- Al-Inshirah 94 — After every hardship comes ease

## 13. Gossip, Backbiting & Slander — الغيبة والنميمة والبهتان (Al-Ghiba wan-Namima)

**English terms:** gossip, backbiting, slander, false accusation, rumour, people talking, defamation  
**Arabic terms:** غيبة، نميمة، بهتان، سوء الظن، لمز، همز، إفك

**Key themes:** the gravity of backbiting, the Ifk episode as a lesson, Allah's protection of the believer's honour

**Anchor verses:**
- Al-Hujurat 49:12 — "O believers, avoid much suspicion... do not backbite one another..."
- An-Nur 24:11-20 — The Ifk episode (slander against Aisha)
- Al-Humaza 104 — Warning against those who gossip and defame
- Al-Hujurat 49:6 — "If a wicked person brings you news, verify it..."

## 14. Backstabbing & Betrayal — الغدر والخيانة (Al-Ghadr wal-Khiyana)

**English terms:** betrayal, backstabbing, treachery, trust broken, deception, injustice from people  
**Arabic terms:** غدر، خيانة، ظلم، مكر، كيد، خذلان

**Key themes:** prophets were betrayed (Yusuf, Dawud, the Prophet ﷺ); Allah sees all; justice will come; the believer's response

**Anchor verses:**
- Yusuf 12:18 — "Patience is most fitting. Allah is the One whose help is sought against what you describe."
- Al-Imran 3:120 — "If something good happens to you they are grieved; if something bad happens they rejoice..."
- Al-Baqarah 2:286 — "Allah does not burden a soul..."
- An-Nisa 4:141 — "Allah will not give the disbelievers a way over the believers."

## 15. Scandal & Public Shaming — الفضيحة والفتنة (Al-Fadihah wal-Fitna)

**English terms:** scandal, public shame, exposed, humiliated, reputation, people judging  
**Arabic terms:** فضيحة، عار، تشهير، فتنة، ستر، يستر الله، عيب

**Key themes:** Allah is As-Sattar (the Concealer), Allah covered the prophets, the Ifk episode's resolution, protection of honour, moving forward after shame

**Anchor verses:**
- An-Nur 24:11-20 — Allah's defence of Aisha after the Ifk slander
- Al-Hujurat 49:11-12 — Do not mock, defame, or call others by bad names
- Az-Zumar 39:53 — "Do not despair of Allah's mercy..." (for those who have sinned and fear exposure)
- Al-Baqarah 2:286 — Reassurance that Allah does not overburden

## 16. Loneliness & Feeling Unseen — الوحدة (Al-Wahda)

**Arabic terms:** وحدة، وحيد، لا أحد، الله معي، قريب، ولا أنا ظالم لأحد

**Key themes:** Allah is always close, Allah hears everything, never truly alone with Allah, the Prophet's loneliness was answered with Surah Duha

**Anchor verses:**
- Qaf 50:16 — "We are closer to him than his jugular vein."
- Al-Baqarah 2:186 — "When My servants ask about Me, I am near..."
- Duha 93 — Divine comfort during a period of silence and loneliness
- Ta-Ha 20:46 — "Fear not. I am with you both, hearing and seeing."

## 20. Fear of Allah / Accountability — الخوف من الله والحساب (Al-Khawf minal-Allah)

**English terms:** fear of Allah, day of judgment, accountability, standing before Allah, taqwa  
**Arabic terms:** خوف، تقوى، يوم الحساب، خشية، وقوف بين يدي الله، ذنوب

**Key themes:** fear paired with hope, taqwa as shield, the Day when nothing is hidden, motivation to change

**Anchor verses:**
- Az-Zalzalah 99:7-8 — "Whoever does an atom's weight of good will see it; whoever does an atom's weight of evil will see it."
- Al-Kahf 18:49 — "They will find everything they did present..."
- Al-Bayyinah 98:8 — "That is for those who fear their Lord."
- Al-Insan 76:10 — "We fear from our Lord a Day of frowning severity."
